# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our pledge
- Use the lab responsibly and only for defensive education.
- Be respectful of other contributors.
- Avoid discriminatory language or behavior.

## Standards
- Demonstrate empathy and kindness in all interactions.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on security hardening and safety.

## Enforcement
- Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers via security contact listed in `SECURITY.md`.
- Violations may result in issue/PR moderation or removal from the project community.
